FAQ

A few helpful answers.

Are Rylami resources therapy?

No. Rylami creates educational and supportive resources. They do not establish a therapist-client relationship or replace individualized mental-health care.

Can therapists or other professionals use Rylami resources?

Some resources are intended to be useful to helping professionals as well as families. The specific license and permitted uses for each product will be stated clearly before purchase.

How will I receive a digital resource?

Delivery details will be added when Rylami’s store and checkout provider are selected and connected.

Can I share or reproduce a purchased resource?

Usage rights will depend on the license attached to the specific product. Rylami will publish clear personal-use and, where offered, professional-use terms before sales begin.
